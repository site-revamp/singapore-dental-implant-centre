# Singapore Dental Implant Centre — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Singapore Dental Implant Centre (SDIC) is a dental practice at Camden Medical Centre, Singapore, offering implant treatment alongside general, restorative and aesthetic dentistry. The website helps adults seeking tooth replacement, restorative treatment or ongoing dental care explore services and request a consultation.

The redesigned homepage presents the clinic’s implant focus and location, provides direct treatment links and explains what happens before treatment begins. Its direction reflects SDIC’s ORAL philosophy: Optimisation, Restoration, Aesthetics and Longevity.

## Three Main Improvements

### 1. A More Relevant Hero Message

The hero combines a descriptive implant headline, the Camden location and a clear consultation action. This addresses the previous emphasis on the practice name and experience statement, helping patients understand the clinic’s offering and location immediately.

### 2. Direct Links to Relevant Treatments

Named treatment links lead to relevant service pages. Previously, all four main service-category “Read More” links led to the same Conditions page. The revised links clarify destinations and make specific treatment information easier to find.

### 3. A Clearer First-Consultation Guide

The homepage explains assessment and discussion before treatment and distinguishes an appointment request from a confirmed booking. This fills the gap in first-visit guidance and provides context before the implant process, which previously began with post placement.

## Design Improvements

- **Visual hierarchy:** The hero prioritises treatment relevance, location and the consultation action.
- **Navigation consistency:** Descriptive treatment links replace generic “Read More” labels and align with their destinations.
- **Content flow:** First-consultation guidance creates a clearer progression from exploring treatments to requesting an appointment.
