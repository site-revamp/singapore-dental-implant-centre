A little clarity before you visit

Questions are
a good place to start.

Bring your questions to the consultation. Your dentist can explain what applies to your individual situation.

Are implants my only option?
Implants are one option for replacing missing teeth. Depending on your needs, your dentist may also discuss bridges or dentures. An assessment helps establish the suitable choices.
How much will treatment cost?
Fees depend on the treatment recommended for you. Ask the clinic about consultation fees and request an explanation of the proposed treatment estimate, including any additional stages.
Can I use MediSave?
The clinic states that it is MediSave accredited. Eligible surgical components may be claimable; restorative components such as crowns are treated differently. Ask the clinic to confirm eligibility and applicable conditions.
What if I feel nervous?
Tell the clinic when requesting your appointment. Use your consultation to discuss your concerns and the options appropriate to your treatment and medical history.
Is my online request a confirmed booking?
No. The clinic’s booking page states that it will contact you to confirm your appointment. You can also telephone or WhatsApp the clinic to discuss arrangements.
